# Public Demo Notice

Advisor Transition Intelligence is an educational decision-support prototype. It is not investment, tax, legal, or retirement advice and does not execute trades.

For the public demo:

- Use synthetic or anonymized information only.
- Do not enter passwords, brokerage credentials, Aadhaar/PAN numbers, bank details, account numbers, or other direct identifiers.
- Results are scenario estimates, not guarantees.
- Tax calculations are simplified and should be reviewed by a qualified professional.
- The demo does not permanently save user-entered portfolio information.

The competition build is configured around the United States problem statement. A production version would need jurisdiction-specific rules, professional review, stronger identity and access controls, encrypted persistence, backups, monitoring, and a formal privacy/compliance program before accepting sensitive personal financial data.
