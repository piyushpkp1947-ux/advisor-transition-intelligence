# Five-minute live demo script

## 0:00–0:30 — The problem

“A higher-return proposal is not automatically better. Taxes, surrender costs, liquidity, risk, and retirement income can reverse the answer. Advisor Transition Intelligence calculates the net client outcome.”

## 0:30–1:15 — Account-aware intake

Open **Portfolio intake**. Point out the account type, tax basis, holding period, surrender cost, and transaction-cost fields. Explain that the model distinguishes taxable, Traditional IRA, Roth IRA, and annuity assets.

## 1:15–2:00 — Make a counterfactual change

Set the **maximum transition-friction budget** to `$0`, then run the analysis. Explain that the product is allowed to recommend keeping the current strategy rather than forcing a transition.

## 2:00–2:45 — Restore realistic constraints

Restore the default budget and run again. In the **Decision cockpit**, show the eligible ranking and explain that eligibility gates are applied before the transparent decision score.

## 2:45–3:30 — Explain friction and staged execution

Show the gain/loss, tax, surrender-cost and transaction-cost breakdown. Then show the tax-efficient lot plan and phased schedule. State clearly that wash-sale, carryforward and contract-rider cases are review gates, not fabricated results.

## 3:30–4:15 — Evidence and resilience

Open **Evidence & stress** and **Retirement resilience**. Explain the historic proxy-data scope, the 2020/2022 stress windows, percentile range, Social Security, inflation, RMD timing, and the fact that these are scenario estimates—not promises.

## 4:15–5:00 — Governance and close

Open **Audit trail**. Show the source provenance, constraints, and advisor acknowledgement. Close with: “The product’s value is not predicting the future; it is making every transition trade-off visible, repeatable, and reviewable.”
